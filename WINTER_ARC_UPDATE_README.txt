FORMFIT — WINTER ARC UPDATE

Added a dedicated Winter Arc page without changing pose, camera, Form Checker, diet backend, auth, or workout backend routes.

Feature scope:
- Winter Arc explainer: what it is, what to do, what not to do.
- Goal/reason/split/training-days/session-duration/food-style setup.
- Explicit equipment selection; Bodyweight is not assumed.
- Habit checklist for training/recovery, nutrition, movement, sleep, and optional screen-free time.
- Build Winter Workout button reuses the existing AI workout generator.
- Build Winter Diet button reuses the existing Meal Plan generator.
- Winter Arc settings are stored locally in the browser for continuity.

Safety note:
Winter Arc is treated as a flexible seasonal consistency framework, not a rigid medical or fitness protocol. The page avoids extreme daily-exercise, starvation, sleep-deprivation, or restart-from-zero rules.
